## Tame for jobs, not collection

Early ARK progression improves fastest when each tame solves a recurring problem.

A good tame should reduce one of these costs:

- Travel time
- Carry weight
- Harvesting time
- Resource weight
- Combat risk
- Cave access
- Breeding preparation

## First useful tame

Your earliest dependable creature should improve either transport or basic gathering.

The exact species matters less than having something you can replace if it dies.

Do not spend hours perfect-taming an early creature whose only job is carrying your first loads of resources.

## Transport

A strong transport tame changes the entire game.

Prioritize:

- Carry weight
- Stamina
- Safe travel speed
- Ability to escape common threats

Transport unlocks safer resource routes and makes base expansion much easier.

## Wood and thatch

Once base construction scales up, manual harvesting becomes inefficient.

A specialized gatherer should dramatically increase the amount you can bring home per trip.

## Stone and metal

Dedicated harvesting creatures are among the biggest progression jumps in ARK.

Your mining loop should eventually become:

1. Travel to resource zone.
2. Harvest with specialized tame.
3. Move large quantities efficiently.
4. Return safely.
5. Process at an organized base.

## Protection tame

Keep a creature whose job is simply to make routine trips safer.

The best utility tame in the world is frustrating if every resource run ends because a predator kills you while you are overloaded.

## Cave tames

Caves have different constraints than the open world.

Before entering, consider:

- Creature size
- Mobility
- Damage
- Survivability
- Cave terrain
- Ability to retreat

Do not assume your strongest outdoor mount is automatically the best cave creature.

## Breeding transition

Once you have stable resource production, begin breeding creatures that will matter long term.

Good breeding targets include:

- Boss army creatures
- Travel mounts
- High-use resource gatherers
- Cave specialists

## Boss preparation

Do not start boss breeding after you decide to fight the boss.

Begin much earlier.

You need time for:

- Good breeding stock
- Stat selection
- Imprinting
- Saddle acquisition
- Leveling
- Replacement animals

## Taming discipline

Ask one question before every major tame:

> What recurring problem does this creature solve?

If the answer is unclear, it may be a collection tame rather than a progression tame.

> Species-specific rankings are intentionally left out of this page until the current ASA map/version is reviewed, because map availability and balance can change.
