## The purpose of breeding

Breeding is most valuable when you stop thinking about “best Pal overall” and start breeding for **jobs**.

Your major projects should be separated into:

- Flying mount
- Ground/utility mount
- Mining worker
- Logging worker
- Handiwork/crafting worker
- Transport worker
- Farming/food worker
- Cooling/heating/electricity specialist
- Combat attacker
- Combat support

## Build one project at a time

Do not run ten breeding projects simultaneously.

For each project:

1. Choose the role.
2. Choose the target species.
3. Identify the passives/traits that improve that role.
4. Breed toward the clean combination.
5. Only then invest heavily in enhancement systems.

This keeps eggs, storage, and breeding stock manageable.

## Base workers

A great base Pal is one that spends most of its time performing the job you actually need.

### Mining

Prioritize:

- High mining suitability
- Work speed
- Low downtime
- Ability to remain on the correct task

### Handiwork

Crafting workers are most valuable when they reduce waiting.

A specialized handiwork Pal can be worth more than a generally strong Pal that constantly leaves the bench to perform unrelated work.

### Transport

Transport efficiency is easy to underestimate.

A base can have excellent production but still feel slow because finished materials sit on the ground or never reach storage.

### Food production

Food bases need a complete chain:

**planting → watering → gathering → transport → cooking/storage**

Improving only one step can simply move the bottleneck to the next step.

## Base specialization

As additional bases become available, stop forcing one location to do everything.

A strong long-term structure is:

### Resource base

Focused on mining/logging and bulk materials.

### Production base

Focused on crafting, assembly, cooking, and electricity.

### Breeding/support base

Focused on breeding, cake/food support, incubation, and storage of breeding stock.

Specialized bases make worker assignments much easier to understand.

## Flying mount project

For a flying mount, decide what matters most:

- Maximum speed
- Stamina
- Combat ability
- General utility

A dedicated travel mount should be bred differently from a combat flyer.

## Combat breeding

Combat Pals should be built around a specific purpose.

Examples:

- General attacker
- Boss counter
- Element specialist
- Tank/support
- Player-damage support

Do not spend maximum enhancement resources on a Pal simply because it is rare. Invest when the Pal has a stable role in your long-term roster.

## Enhancement order

A useful discipline is:

1. Get the correct species.
2. Get the correct role/passives.
3. Confirm it will actually be used.
4. Improve IV/stat quality where relevant.
5. Condense/essence-enhance.
6. Invest other rare permanent resources.

Permanent investment should come after breeding quality, not before.

## Breeding stock

Keep breeders for valuable traits even when the individual Pal is not useful in combat.

Organize breeding stock separately from:

- Workers
- Combat team
- Mounts
- Sell/release candidates

A clean breeding box saves enormous time later.

## When to stop optimizing

Do not chase perfection when the current worker or fighter already solves the problem easily.

Perfect breeding makes sense for:

- Long-term mounts
- Endgame combat
- Workers used constantly
- Pals that feed future breeding chains

It matters much less for a temporary worker you will replace soon.
