## First-base objective

Your first base needs to support storage, crafting, food, water, and safe recovery. It does not need to become the final Blood Moon fortress.

## Location priorities

Favor:

- Reasonable access to a trader and roads
- Multiple nearby POIs
- Basic resource access
- Defensible terrain
- Enough open area to expand
- A route that does not force you through high-threat zones every day

## Home base versus horde base

Separating your main storage/crafting base from the structure used for Blood Moons reduces the chance that one failed defense destroys your entire progression.

## First week

Focus on:

1. Reliable food and water
2. Storage organization
3. Workstation progression
4. Better tools
5. Transportation
6. Ammo and repair supplies
7. A simple, tested horde defense

## Navezgane mindset

Use roads and recognizable landmarks to create repeatable loot circuits. The map becomes much easier once travel is planned around known routes rather than random wandering.
