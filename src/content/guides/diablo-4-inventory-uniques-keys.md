## Uniques

Keep an unused unique when at least one of these is true:

- It is part of the Season 15 Blazing Scream target setup.
- It enables another build you realistically expect to play.
- It has an unusually strong roll compared with your current copy.
- It is difficult enough to replace that reacquiring it would be annoying.
- It is useful for a known alternate Season 15 variant.

### Blazing Scream pieces that should not be casually salvaged

Keep functional or strong copies of:

- Elegy
- Infernal Homunculus
- Eightfold Idol
- Temerity
- Moloch's Beating Flame
- Hand of Apotheosis
- Leoric's Crown
- Stone of Jordan

Also keep the correct pieces needed for:

- Enigma
- Undying Aspect boots
- Sadistic Aspect setup

## Duplicates

For duplicate uniques:

1. Compare the new copy directly with the equipped copy.
2. Keep the stronger roll.
3. Keep a second copy only if it serves a real alternate setup.
4. Salvage or otherwise convert clearly inferior extras.

Do not keep five mediocre copies of the same unique “just in case.”

The stash should contain future options, not every drop that has a unique border.

## Legendary / rare gear

Keep a non-unique item when it has:

- The correct base
- The affixes needed for the current build
- Clear upgrade potential
- A role in a known alternate Season 15 setup

Do not keep gear only because the item power is high.

A lower-looking item that preserves the build interaction can be more valuable than a nominal numerical upgrade that breaks the setup.

## Keys

Keys and boss-access materials have more value when your build is strong enough to convert them into fast, efficient kills.

### Keep keys when

- The target boss drops an item you still need
- You are building a batch for efficient boss farming
- The key is tied to a Season 15 progression target you have not completed

### Do not waste keys when

- The boss is still a slow, difficult kill
- You do not need anything meaningful from that target
- You are better off finishing glyphs / gear first

## Tributes / Sigils

### Tributes

Keep valuable Tributes until you know what resource or reward you want to target.

Use them intentionally rather than consuming them simply because you have them.

Refinement Tributes are especially relevant when **Forgotten Souls** are the current bottleneck.

### Sigil Dust / Nightmare Sigils

Season 15 Key Caches can help supply Sigil Dust.

Keep enough Sigil resources to support the Nightmare Dungeon runs you need for Obducite.

Do not overstock low-value clutter once the relevant progression need has passed.

## Runes

Do not casually discard Legacy Runes required for the build.

The Enigma target requires:

- Jah
- Ith
- Ber

The build's active rune pairs also include:

- Nagu + Tir
- Cir + Mot
- Igni + Tir as the noted alternative first pair

If a rune is part of one of those targets, it has an obvious use.

## Gems

Once you are using the target setup, keep enough materials to maintain:

- Royal Ruby in the weapon
- Royal Sapphire in armor
- Royal Ruby in jewelry

Do not fill stash space with large quantities of lower-tier gems that no longer serve a realistic upgrade path.

## Stash layout

Use functional tabs/categories.

### Tab 1 — Current Blazing Scream upgrades

Only pieces that could replace what you are actually wearing.

### Tab 2 — Alternate Season 15 build pieces

Strong uniques or gear for a build you genuinely may switch to.

### Tab 3 — Boss / access materials

Keys and materials used for targeted farming.

### Tab 4 — Runes / build components

Especially Enigma runes and active rune-pair pieces.

### Tab 5 — Experimental / high-roll uniques

Only items strong enough to justify keeping despite not having an immediate use.

## When to salvage aggressively

Become stricter as the build matures.

Early endgame:
- Keep more possibilities because the final setup is incomplete.

Mid progression:
- Keep only pieces that can realistically beat the current item.

Late progression:
- Salvage almost anything that is not a direct upgrade, an important alternate, or a difficult-to-replace high roll.

A clean stash is part of progression because it makes every loot session faster to evaluate.

## Fast decision rule

When an item drops, ask:

1. Is it part of my Season 15 target setup?
2. Is it better than my current copy?
3. Does it enable a real alternate build?
4. Is it unusually hard to reacquire?
5. Do I know exactly why I am keeping it?

If all five answers are no, it probably does not need stash space.
