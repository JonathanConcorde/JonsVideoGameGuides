## The basic rule

Your stash should hold **future options**, not every item that looks interesting.

## Uniques

Keep an unused unique when at least one of these is true:

- It enables a build you may realistically play.
- It has an unusually strong roll.
- It is difficult enough to replace that reacquiring it would be annoying.
- It is part of a likely alternate setup for your current build.

Salvage or otherwise convert duplicates that are clearly worse than the copy you already own and have no alternate-build purpose.

## Legendary and rare gear

Keep items that have the correct base, strong key affixes, or clear upgrade potential. Do not keep mediocre pieces merely because they are high item power.

## Keys and access items

Hold onto keys and access materials until you understand which endgame activity consumes them and whether that activity is part of your current progression plan. Avoid burning limited-entry resources while your build is still too weak to complete the content efficiently.

## A simple stash system

Create functional groups:

- Current build alternatives
- Future build uniques
- Boss or activity keys
- Crafting and upgrade pieces
- Experimental items

If an item does not fit one of those groups, it probably does not need permanent stash space.

## When to salvage aggressively

Once your build is stable, become stricter. The farther you progress, the more valuable clean inventory and crafting materials become compared with low-probability “maybe someday” gear.
