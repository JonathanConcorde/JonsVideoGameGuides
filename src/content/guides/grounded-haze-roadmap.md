## Before entering the Haze

The Haze is a preparation check as much as a combat zone.

Do not walk in simply because the quest points there.

Your checklist should cover:

- Environmental protection
- Healing
- Food and water
- Weapon durability
- Armor durability
- Light
- Repair materials
- Inventory space
- A nearby respawn/recovery plan

## Establish a nearby staging point

Create a safe point outside the Haze where you can:

- Save/respawn
- Store extra supplies
- Repair
- Refill food and water
- Stage a corpse-recovery attempt

The closer this staging point is without being exposed to the environmental hazard, the better.

## Weapon planning

Carry a reliable primary weapon plus an alternate option when possible.

The important rule is not to arrive with one powerful weapon at half durability and no repair path.

## Healing

Bring more healing than you expect to need.

Environmental damage, unfamiliar enemies, and bad terrain can compound quickly. A small mistake that would be harmless elsewhere can become lethal when several threats overlap.

## Movement discipline

Do not spend all stamina attacking.

Keep enough stamina available to:

- Escape terrain
- Break line of sight
- Reach clean air/safety
- Avoid a dangerous enemy attack

## During the Haze

Explore in loops.

1. Enter with full supplies.
2. Complete one clear objective.
3. Mark useful resources.
4. Return to the staging point.
5. Repair and refill.
6. Re-enter.

Trying to fully clear an unfamiliar region in one trip creates unnecessary corpse-recovery risk.

## After completing the Haze milestone

Do not immediately sprint to the next major objective.

Use the new progression unlocks to improve the systems that were weakest during the Haze.

Review:

- Weapon tier
- Armor
- Food
- Healing production
- Base storage
- Zipline/mobility options
- Resource routes
- Upgrade materials

## Post-Haze priorities

### 1. Upgrade the weapon you actually use

Do not spread rare materials across every weapon.

Build one reliable primary combat setup first.

### 2. Improve food

Better food effectively improves every combat encounter because it increases how much room you have for mistakes.

### 3. Expand mobility

As the yard opens up, travel time becomes a major cost.

Invest in routes that make repeated resource runs easier.

### 4. Build resource stockpiles

The next progression tier becomes much smoother if common materials are already stored instead of gathered only when a recipe requires them.

### 5. Prepare for the next lab like a new biome

Do not assume the same loadout that survived the Haze will solve the next region.

Check the new enemies and environmental challenge before committing.

## Recovery rule

If you cannot explain how you will recover your backpack after dying in a location, you are not fully prepared to enter it.
