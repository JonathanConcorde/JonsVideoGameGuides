## Early game

Your first objective is not to build a perfect base. Build a base that lets you automate the chores preventing exploration.

Prioritize:

- Reliable food
- Wood and stone production
- Storage close to work areas
- Basic crafting automation
- A small combat roster
- A dependable early mount

## Base Pal philosophy

Assign Pals according to the work you need done most often, not simply by overall rarity. A lower-tier Pal with the right work suitability can be more useful than a stronger combat Pal sitting idle.

## Midgame transition

As better technology unlocks, separate your needs into three systems:

1. **Resource base** — mining, logging, bulk materials.
2. **Production base** — crafting, cooking, assembly, medicine.
3. **Breeding/support base** — breeding, food support, and specialized production.

## Combat roster

Build for coverage rather than one favorite Pal. Keep strong options for boss fights, mobility, and difficult elemental matchups.

## Breeding

Treat breeding as a way to combine access, passives, and role specialization. The long-term targets are usually:

- Strong flying mount
- Fast ground or utility mount
- Optimized base workers
- Dedicated combat Pals

## Late game

The late game becomes an optimization loop: better passives, better breeding chains, better base throughput, stronger equipment, and more efficient access to rare materials.
