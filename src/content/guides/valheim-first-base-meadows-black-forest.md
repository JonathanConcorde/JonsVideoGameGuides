## Why this location is strong

A Meadows bay beside Black Forest gives you a useful combination: safe early building ground, water access, nearby wood and ore progression, and a natural transition into exploration by boat.

## Where to place the actual house

Build firmly inside the Meadows rather than directly on the biome boundary. You want Black Forest resources close without inviting constant higher-threat enemies into the work area.

## Early layout

Reserve separate space for:

- Main house and rested-bonus area
- Workbench and forge expansion
- Cooking
- Storage
- Smelting and charcoal
- Portal area
- Dock
- Farming
- Defensive perimeter

Leave more room than you think you need. Early bases become cramped quickly once metal processing and portal traffic increase.

## The shoreline

Use the bay as infrastructure, not decoration. Create an obvious landing area with chests nearby for ore and expedition supplies. Keep the boat route clear and avoid forcing yourself to carry heavy loads uphill through the entire base.

## Black Forest access

Create one safe, repeatable path from the base toward the Forest. Mark copper, burial chambers, troll caves, and useful coastlines. The goal is to turn repeated trips into known logistics rather than fresh exploration every time.

## Defenses

Do not overbuild fortifications immediately. Start with terrain awareness, lighting, clear sight lines, and a perimeter that stops ordinary enemies from wandering directly into work areas. Upgrade defenses as raids and biome pressure increase.

## When to move

Do not move just because you unlock the next biome. A good coastal Meadows base can remain the main home for a long time if it has enough building room, water access, and portal space.
