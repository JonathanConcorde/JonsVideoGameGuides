## Build objective

The build should make combat feel like a sequence of controlled entries:

1. Identify the dangerous targets.
2. Activate time-slow.
3. Close distance.
4. Remove priority enemies.
5. Reposition before normal-speed crossfire becomes dangerous.
6. Repeat.

A good katana/Sandevistan build is not simply “maximum melee damage.” It combines **damage, movement, defense, and uptime**.

## Early game

Before the full Sandevistan setup is available, make blades reliable.

Prioritize:

- Blade perks
- Stamina efficiency
- Movement
- Health/survivability
- Cyberware capacity
- A dependable katana rather than constantly swapping weapons for tiny stat gains

Use cover and positioning more than the final build eventually will.

## Early cyberware

Look for cyberware that solves real problems:

- Getting into melee range
- Surviving the first few seconds of contact
- Recovering after a kill
- Improving armor
- Increasing mobility
- Supporting cooldown recovery

Do not fill every slot with damage if the character dies while crossing open space.

## Midgame transition

The build changes once time-slow becomes available often enough to structure fights around it.

Your new goal is to make each activation accomplish something important.

### Good activation

You enter, remove several priority enemies, and exit/reposition before the battlefield recovers.

### Bad activation

You trigger time-slow while too far away, spend most of it running, and reach the first target as the effect ends.

## Target priority

A useful default order:

1. Enemy that can disable or heavily disrupt you
2. Dangerous ranged target
3. High-damage elite
4. Isolated target that can be removed instantly
5. Low-threat cleanup

You are using time manipulation to control the encounter, so spend it on the enemies that make the encounter dangerous.

## Mobility

Movement is both offense and defense.

Use mobility perks/cyberware to:

- Reach elevated enemies
- Cross firing lanes
- Avoid being surrounded
- Move between targets without wasting the Sandevistan window
- Break contact after the activation ends

## Katana selection

Choose the weapon that reinforces the actual build loop.

A katana with a useful interaction for your playstyle can be better than a nominally stronger weapon whose benefit rarely matters.

Evaluate:

- Damage
- Attack speed
- Crit/synergy
- Unique effect
- Interaction with movement
- Interaction with time-slow

## Late game

At high investment, the build should feel increasingly continuous.

The major optimization targets become:

- Sandevistan duration
- Cooldown/recovery
- Cyberware synergy
- Defensive layers
- Movement
- Katana-specific damage
- Kill-to-kill momentum

## Boss strategy

Do not assume the same “delete the room” pattern works on bosses.

Against durable enemies:

1. Activate for a clean damage phase.
2. Avoid overcommitting when the window ends.
3. Reposition.
4. Recover cooldowns/resources.
5. Repeat.

The build stays aggressive, but boss fights reward repeated good entries rather than one reckless all-in attack.

## Attribute/perk philosophy

When choosing between two upgrades, favor the one that improves the full combat loop.

A perk that makes movement, stamina, or survivability reliable can add more real damage than a small raw-damage increase because it keeps you attacking.
