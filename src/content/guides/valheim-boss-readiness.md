## The readiness test

Finding the altar does not mean you are ready.

The best signal is that the biome itself has become manageable.

If normal exploration still feels like a desperate expedition, keep preparing.

## Food

Bring your best practical food, not food you are afraid to use.

A boss attempt is exactly when premium food should be consumed.

Your food setup should support the fight you expect:

- More health if you need mistake tolerance
- More stamina if movement/attacking is the limitation
- Balanced setup for unfamiliar mechanics

## Rested bonus

Do not start a boss fight with a nearly expired rested timer.

Refresh it immediately beforehand.

The stamina and regeneration benefits can matter throughout the entire fight.

## Gear

Before summoning:

- Repair everything.
- Upgrade the main weapon if reasonable.
- Upgrade armor.
- Bring ammunition if using ranged attacks.
- Bring a backup weapon when durability could matter.

## Resistances and consumables

If the biome or boss has a known environmental/damage threat, prepare the appropriate resistance or protection.

Do not rely on healing to compensate for avoidable damage.

## Arena preparation

Before summoning the boss:

- Clear nearby enemies.
- Understand the terrain.
- Remove obstacles that trap movement.
- Create escape space.
- Mark the altar.
- Establish a nearby portal or safe staging point when possible.

## Recovery setup

Assume you may die.

Keep outside the fight:

- Spare food
- Spare armor
- Spare weapon
- Portal access
- A safe bed/respawn point

A failed boss attempt should not become a multi-hour recovery crisis.

## When to delay the fight

Delay if:

- Your best gear is damaged and cannot be repaired nearby.
- You are short on food.
- Your rested bonus is poor.
- You have no recovery route.
- Regular biome enemies still overwhelm you.
- Your weapon damage feels ineffective against normal enemies.

There is little value in forcing an underprepared attempt.

## During the fight

Prioritize survival over squeezing in one extra attack.

Most boss deaths happen because the player spends the stamina or positioning needed to avoid the next mechanic.

Attack during clean openings.

Move during danger.

Recover stamina when neither is safe.

## After the kill

Do not immediately move all operations to the next biome.

Use the new boss progression unlock to:

1. Improve the current base.
2. Craft the newly available tools/items.
3. Restock food and consumables.
4. Scout the next biome.
5. Establish a foothold before risking the main equipment set.
