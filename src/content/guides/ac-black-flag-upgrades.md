## Core rule

Upgrade what removes progression friction. The best early upgrades are the ones that make naval fights, boarding, and resource acquisition easier enough to fund the next round of upgrades.

## Jackdaw priorities

Prioritize practical combat power and survivability before cosmetic or marginal improvements.

A good general order is:

1. Hull durability
2. Broadside effectiveness
3. Heavy-shot capability
4. Mortar utility
5. Swivel effectiveness
6. Storage when resource caps begin to interfere with progress

## Why survivability matters

A stronger hull gives you more room for mistakes and makes difficult naval encounters less punishing. That extra survivability often produces more resources because you can safely finish and board fights you would otherwise abandon.

## Side content

Do side activities when they unlock meaningful plans, resources, or quality-of-life improvements. Do not feel obligated to clear every icon before advancing the story.

## Boarding

Boarding is central to the economy. Use it to repair the Jackdaw and accumulate the materials needed for the next meaningful upgrade.

## Upgrade-plan rule

If a plan requires an awkward detour, ask whether the resulting upgrade changes what you can successfully fight. If not, it can usually wait.
