## Core philosophy

Progress deliberately: secure food, rested bonus, upgraded equipment, reliable portals, and a defensible base before pushing deeply into a new biome.

## Meadows

Establish shelter, a bed, cooking, storage, and dependable food. Explore the coastline and mark useful resources before committing to longer expeditions.

## Black Forest

Prioritize bronze production, dungeon exploration, core wood, and portal infrastructure. Avoid letting early ore runs become unnecessarily dangerous by creating organized transport routes.
