## Core philosophy

Progress deliberately. Valheim punishes entering the next biome before your food, rested bonus, equipment, and escape plan are ready.

The basic loop is:

1. Stabilize the current biome.
2. Upgrade the tools and gear that unlock the next step.
3. Build repeatable resource routes.
4. Prepare food and consumables.
5. Scout the next biome from safety.
6. Establish a foothold.
7. Fight the boss only when the biome itself feels manageable.

## Meadows

Your first objectives are shelter, bed, cooking, storage, a workbench, reliable food, and a safe respawn point.

Explore enough coastline to understand your starting island. Mark useful resources and good future building locations rather than committing immediately to the first flat clearing.

### Before leaving the Meadows phase

You should have:

- Reliable food
- Upgraded basic equipment
- Multiple storage chests
- A comfortable rested-bonus routine
- A mapped path to Black Forest
- A base location with room to expand

## Black Forest

This is where logistics become important.

Priorities:

- Burial Chambers
- Surtling Cores
- Core Wood
- Copper and Tin
- Bronze production
- Better weapons and tools
- Portal infrastructure

Do not turn every ore run into a new expedition. Mark deposits and create repeatable routes.

## The Elder checkpoint

Before fighting the Elder, make sure the Black Forest no longer feels like an uncontrolled threat. You should be able to travel, mine, and clear common enemies without using all of your food and healing just to stay alive.

## Swamp preparation

The Swamp is a major difficulty increase because danger comes from terrain, visibility, poison, stamina pressure, and enemies at the same time.

Before committing deeply:

- Improve food
- Bring poison protection
- Repair and upgrade gear
- Establish a nearby portal outside immediate danger
- Carry materials for emergency shelter and workbench access
- Avoid exploring at low rested time

## Swamp progression

Your objective is to turn the Swamp from a hostile expedition zone into a repeatable resource route. Once crypt access, iron transport, food, and portal logistics are stable, the biome becomes much less punishing.

## Mountains and beyond

From this point forward, keep using the same preparation model:

**environmental hazard → required protection → resource unlock → gear upgrade → boss preparation**

Do not rush a boss because you have found the altar. The strongest signal that you are ready is that normal exploration in the biome has become controlled.

## Base strategy

A single good coastal Meadows base can remain your primary home for a long time. Use portals and specialized outposts rather than moving everything every time a new biome opens.

## Death recovery rule

Every dangerous trip should answer one question before you leave:

> If I die there, how am I getting my gear back?

That means spare food, spare equipment, portal access, and a known route are progression tools—not luxuries.
