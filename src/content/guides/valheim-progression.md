## Progression order

Valheim 1.0 now has a complete biome progression:

1. Meadows
2. Black Forest
3. Swamp
4. Mountains
5. Plains
6. Mistlands
7. Ashlands
8. Deep North

The central rule does not change: **make the current biome routine before forcing the next one.**

## Core preparation loop

Before entering a new biome seriously:

- Upgrade food.
- Repair and upgrade equipment.
- Refresh the rested bonus.
- Carry portal materials when appropriate.
- Create a recovery plan before you need one.
- Scout the biome edge before moving your entire operation.
- Identify the environmental hazard unique to that biome.

## Meadows

Use the Meadows to establish the systems that make everything else easier.

Priorities:

- Shelter and bed
- Workbench upgrades
- Cooking
- Storage
- Bow and basic weapons
- Reliable food
- Coastal exploration
- A base with expansion room

A good Meadows base can remain the central home for much of the game.

## Black Forest

This is the first real logistics phase.

Prioritize:

- Burial Chambers
- Surtling Cores
- Core Wood
- Copper
- Tin
- Bronze
- Forge upgrades
- Portals

Your goal is to turn mining and dungeon trips into repeatable routes rather than dangerous one-off expeditions.

## Swamp

The Swamp combines poor terrain, stamina pressure, poison, visibility problems, and dangerous enemies.

Before deep exploration:

- Bring poison protection.
- Upgrade food.
- Establish a portal outside the most dangerous area.
- Bring a hoe when terrain management can help.
- Keep enough stamina to move rather than spending everything attacking.

Iron is the key progression resource. Once crypt runs and iron transport become routine, prepare for Bonemass.

## Mountains

The Mountains add environmental damage to the normal exploration problem.

Prepare cold protection before treating the biome as a destination. Build around:

- Safe ascent/descent routes
- Silver extraction
- Better food
- Strong ranged options
- Emergency shelter

Do not let a corpse run become your first serious Mountain expedition.

## Plains

The Plains punish careless movement even when your equipment looks strong.

Priorities include:

- Better food production
- Barley/flax infrastructure when available
- Black metal progression
- Safer settlement routes
- Controlled exploration instead of sprinting across open ground

## Mistlands

Visibility and terrain become part of the challenge.

The Mistlands phase is less about raw combat power and more about maintaining control in an environment where navigation is difficult.

Plan short, repeatable expeditions. Mark useful locations aggressively and avoid carrying every valuable item on exploratory trips.

## Ashlands

Ashlands expects mature logistics.

Before committing:

- Have a reliable portal and transport strategy.
- Bring strong food and fully upgraded equipment.
- Carry enough supplies to establish a foothold.
- Avoid treating the first landing as a sightseeing trip.

Ashlands is most manageable when you turn the hostile coast into a stable operational zone.

## Deep North

Deep North is the final biome added with Valheim 1.0.

Treat entry into the Deep North as an endgame expedition, not simply the next map color. By this point your preparation should include:

- Top-tier food
- Fully developed crafting infrastructure
- Strong backup equipment
- Multiple portal options
- Redundant recovery supplies
- A clear logistics chain back to your main base

Do not rush directly from an Ashlands victory into Deep North exploration with a depleted inventory.

## Boss readiness

A boss altar is not a readiness indicator.

A better test is:

> Can I explore this biome, gather its normal resources, and survive common enemies without consuming all of my emergency supplies?

If the answer is no, continue improving the biome loop before fighting the boss.

## Death recovery

Before every dangerous trip, know:

1. Where you will respawn.
2. Which spare food you will use.
3. Which spare equipment you will wear.
4. How you will reach the body.
5. Which portal or boat route will get you there.

Recovery planning is part of progression.
