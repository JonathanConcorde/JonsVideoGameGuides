## Overall objective

Build an outpost network that produces useful manufactured components continuously while you quest, then convert those materials into both XP and credits when you return.

## Before committing to the network

Prioritize the research and skills that reduce friction in outpost construction, resource extraction, cargo links, storage, and advanced manufacturing.

## Network design

Do not create independent bases with no purpose. Each outpost should have a job:

- Extract a small group of local resources
- Feed one higher-level manufacturing hub
- Act as a logistics transfer point
- Supply a specific advanced component chain

## Central manufacturing hub

Choose one location with enough build space and a useful local resource mix. This becomes the place where incoming materials are stored, routed, and turned into progressively higher-value components.

## Build from simple to complex

Start with reliable low-tier components. Once the storage and cargo network works consistently, layer more complex recipes onto it. Fix bottlenecks before adding another production tier.

## XP loop

The network is most valuable when it works while you are doing other content. Return periodically, collect accumulated resources, manufacture in large batches, and then go back to questing.

## Long-term rule

A slightly less “perfect” planet with easy logistics can outperform a theoretical maximum-efficiency setup that is difficult to maintain. Build for reliability first.
