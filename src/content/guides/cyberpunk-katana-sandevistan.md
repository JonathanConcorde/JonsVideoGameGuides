## Build identity

The build is built around entering combat on your terms, slowing time, closing distance quickly, deleting high-priority targets, and moving before enemies can recover.

## Early priorities

Invest first in the systems that make melee reliable:

- Blade damage and efficiency
- Mobility
- Stamina management
- Defensive tools
- Cyberware capacity and survivability

## Sandevistan role

Do not treat the Sandevistan as a panic button. Use it proactively to create an opening, isolate threats, and prevent enemies from establishing crossfire.

## Combat sequence

1. Identify ranged and high-threat targets.
2. Trigger time-slow before entering the most dangerous space.
3. Close distance immediately.
4. Remove priority targets.
5. Use movement to avoid being surrounded when normal speed returns.

## Equipment

Prioritize weapons and cyberware that improve the build's actual loop rather than generic item score. Mobility, melee performance, survivability, and Sandevistan uptime all reinforce each other.

## Bosses

Against durable targets, shift from pure burst toward repeated controlled windows. Enter, deal damage, disengage or defend while systems recover, then repeat.
