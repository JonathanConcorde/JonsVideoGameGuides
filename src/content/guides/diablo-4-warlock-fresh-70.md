## The goal at level 70

Once the Warlock reaches 70, stop treating every upgrade as equally important. The priority becomes **build function first, efficiency second, min-maxing third**.

## Priority order

| Priority | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Make the core build function reliably |
| 2 | Secure build-defining uniques and aspects |
| 3 | Bring key glyphs online |
| 4 | Fix weak affixes and defensive gaps |
| 5 | Improve gems and socketing |
| 6 | Masterwork the pieces least likely to be replaced |
| 7 | Push higher Torment/Pit content only when clear speed remains efficient |

## First farming loop

Use the highest difficulty where normal packs die quickly and bosses do not turn into long attrition fights. Rotate between activities that solve different problems instead of repeating one activity indefinitely.

- Need build-enabling gear: prioritize the activity that targets those drops.
- Need glyph power: run the content that advances glyphs efficiently.
- Need broad gear volume: favor fast repeatable content.
- Need crafting materials: target the activity that produces the material actually blocking your next upgrade.

## When to increase difficulty

Move up when your current tier feels routine, your survivability is stable, and elites do not force repeated defensive resets. If a higher tier dramatically slows your clears, step back down and farm faster.

## Upgrade discipline

Do not spend premium resources heavily on temporary pieces. A good rule is:

1. Functional placeholder.
2. Correct base item with correct important stats.
3. Strong roll.
4. Masterworking and expensive optimization.

That order prevents wasting materials on an item you replace ten minutes later.

## Boss versus farming setup

For general clearing, favor consistency, mobility, and resource flow. For bosses, prioritize sustained damage, reliable debuff uptime, defensive timing, and clean execution of the build's burst window.

## What to track next

Keep a short list of only the items that meaningfully change the build. Once the core setup is stable, the game becomes much easier to manage because most drops can be judged against a clear target rather than evaluated from scratch.
