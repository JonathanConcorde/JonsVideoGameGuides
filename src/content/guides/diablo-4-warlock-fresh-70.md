## First priorities

Once the Warlock hits 70, stop treating every upgrade as equal.

Use this order:

1. **Get the Blazing Scream build functioning**
2. **Target-farm the build-enabling uniques**
3. **Push Pit for Glyphs + Paragon**
4. **Overlap Whispers with whatever else you are doing**
5. **Use Undercity for targeted rewards / Paragon / materials**
6. **Run Nightmare Dungeons when Obducite is the bottleneck**
7. **Run Helltide for keys and materials**
8. **Move into boss / Mythic farming once the build is stable**

The goal is to solve the current bottleneck instead of farming one activity indefinitely.

## Activity order

### 1. Build-enabling uniques first

Before chasing perfect rolls or expensive masterworking, complete the core item package from the master guide.

If you are missing Elegy, Infernal Homunculus, Eightfold Idol, Temerity, Moloch's Beating Flame, Hand of Apotheosis, Leoric's Crown, Stone of Jordan, Enigma, or the correct boot setup, those gaps generally matter more than polishing secondary stats.

### 2. Pit for Glyphs + Paragon

Blazing Scream is especially good at turning fast Pit clears into efficient progression.

Prioritize Pit when:

- Your key glyphs are behind
- Paragon progression is lagging
- Your current tier clears quickly
- You are not currently blocked by a specific gear target

### 3. Overlap Whispers

Do not ignore Whispers while running other content.

Season 15 Whispers are part of the progression loop because they add useful XP, gold, and materials on top of activities you already wanted to complete.

### 4. Undercity

Use Undercity when you want:

- Targeted reward opportunities
- Paragon progression
- Material-focused runs
- Refinement Tribute value when Forgotten Souls are the bottleneck

### 5. Nightmare Dungeons

Use NMDs with a purpose.

The main reason in this route is **Obducite**.

If you are not currently blocked by Obducite, do not default to NMDs just because they are available.

### 6. Helltide

Use Helltide when you need:

- Keys
- Materials
- Efficient overlap with the Diablo War Plan
- Additional progression while chasing Season 15 resources

### 7. Boss / Mythic farming

Boss and Mythic farming becomes more efficient once the build is already functioning well.

Do not burn a large stockpile of access materials while the character still takes too long to kill the target.

## War Plans

Season 15 War Plans should match your current need.

### Diablo

**Helltide + Hordes**

Use when those activities are already the best route to the materials or progress you need.

### Baal

**Undercity + Lair Bosses**

Use when targeted unique or boss farming is the current priority.

### Mephisto

**Pit + Nightmare Dungeons**

This is the natural choice when Glyphs, Paragon, Pit climbing, or Obducite are your main bottlenecks.

## Pit

### What the Pit is doing for this build

Pit is not merely a push leaderboard.

For this progression route it is a repeatable source of:

- Glyph advancement
- Paragon progress
- Efficient XP when clears are fast

### How hard should you push?

Use the highest tier you can clear efficiently.

A higher tier is not automatically better if:

- Elites take too long
- Bosses stall the run
- You die frequently
- The completion time becomes much worse

Fast repeatable clears usually outperform slow prestige clears for progression.

### Glyph target

Bring the important glyphs toward **50**, then push them through the **50/51 breakpoint**, rather than maxing the first glyph while the rest stay far behind.

Priority:

1. Eldritch Sight
2. Demonologist
3. Unbound
4. Hellforge
5. Attrition

## Whispers

Treat Whispers as an overlay.

If two activities are otherwise similar, favor the one that also completes a Whisper.

The prior Season 15 route specifically valued **Key Caches** because they can add:

- Tributes
- Sigil Dust
- Possible Mephisto Keys / Traces

## Undercity

Undercity is useful when you want more targeted control over what the run is accomplishing.

Use it when:

- The reward category lines up with your current bottleneck
- You need Paragon progress
- You need materials
- A Refinement Tribute solves a Forgotten Soul shortage

Do not spend valuable Tributes blindly. Match the Tribute to an actual current need.

## Nightmare Dungeons

NMDs are primarily an **Obducite stop** in this route.

Run them when masterworking is being held back specifically by Obducite.

Once that bottleneck is solved, go back to the activity that provides the next biggest gain.

## Helltide

Use Helltide for:

- Keys
- Materials
- Diablo War Plan progress
- General resource accumulation

Helltide is most efficient when it overlaps with other objectives rather than becoming an isolated grind.

## Boss / Mythic farming

Save serious boss farming for when the build can kill efficiently.

Use boss runs to target the exact named pieces from the master guide:

- Lord Zir → Elegy
- Urivar → Infernal Homunculus
- Beast in the Ice → Eightfold Idol
- Harbinger of Hatred → Temerity
- Bloodied Butcher → Moloch's Beating Flame
- Bartuc → Hand of Apotheosis
- Baal / Waking Nightmares → Leoric's Crown and Stone of Jordan

### Mythic progression

Season 15 also includes Mythic progression systems that become more relevant once the core build is already stable.

Do not let Mythic chasing distract from a missing build-defining unique or badly underleveled glyph board.

## When to move up

Increase difficulty when the current tier feels routine.

Good signs:

- Packs die quickly
- Elites are not stalling
- Bosses are controlled
- You are not dying often
- The higher tier does not destroy your clear speed

If the next tier makes every activity dramatically slower, step back down and progress faster.

## What should I do next?

Use the Season 15 tracker on the Diablo hub.

The ideal behavior is:

- Missing build item → target that item
- Core gear mostly complete, glyphs behind → Pit
- Forgotten Souls missing → Undercity / Refinement
- Obducite missing → NMD
- Keys/materials missing → Helltide
- Everything stable → bosses / Mythics / higher Pit
