## First objective

Stabilize before you expand. Your first hours should produce a repeatable loop for food, water, storage, crafting, and safe respawning.

## Early tames

Prioritize creatures that solve jobs:

- Carrying
- Harvesting
- Travel
- Protection
- Specialized resource gathering

A utility tame that halves the time needed for a resource can advance you more than an early combat tame.

## Base progression

Build your first serious base where several needs overlap: water, common resources, reasonable terrain, and safe access to the next progression zone.

## Equipment progression

Move through equipment tiers because they unlock safer access to harder zones. Avoid treating armor or weapons as isolated goals; each upgrade should enable a new resource, tame, cave, or region.

## Midgame

The midgame is about infrastructure:

- Dedicated resource tames
- Breeding
- Refrigeration and food preservation
- Better transport
- Organized storage
- Outposts near specialized resources

## Boss preparation

Boss readiness requires more than player gear. Build breeding lines, saddles, consumables, and repeatable resource production well before you intend to enter arenas.
