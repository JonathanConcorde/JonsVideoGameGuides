## Early priorities

Secure food, water, a defensible shelter, basic crafting, and a reliable respawn point. Explore enough to unlock systems, but do not scatter resources across too many unfinished projects.

## Equipment rule

Upgrade gear when it unlocks access to the next objective or materially improves survival. Avoid spending rare upgrade materials on equipment you are about to replace.

## Laboratory progression

Treat each major lab as a preparation checkpoint. Before entering, review:

- Damage type
- Armor
- Healing
- Food and water
- Light source
- Repair materials
- Escape route

## Before the Haze

Prepare for environmental danger in addition to combat. Build enough healing and durability to survive mistakes, not just the ideal route.

## After the Haze

Reassess your base, weapon coverage, resource routes, and mobility. The next part of the game becomes easier when earlier logistics are already automated or familiar.

## Exploration

Mark rare resources and dangerous encounters. Grounded rewards returning to known locations once new tools unlock previously inaccessible materials.
