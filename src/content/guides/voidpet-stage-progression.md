## Current progression point

This guide is being maintained around the **1440+ stage range** and is intended to extend at least the next 100 stages as progression continues.

The priority is **reliable boss clearing first**, with faster or higher-risk alternatives noted only when they offer a meaningful advantage.

## How to invest pets

Do not level every interesting pet equally. Concentrate resources into pets that satisfy at least one of these conditions:

- Core member of several boss teams
- Best answer to a common boss element
- Strong manual-control value
- Useful in both progression and hard-mode training
- High likelihood of remaining relevant for the next major stage band

## Manual control

Manual control should be used to improve consistency rather than simply increase actions per minute.

During boss fights:

1. Identify the dangerous boss action.
2. Preserve your important defensive or disruption tool for that moment.
3. Avoid wasting high-value skills on low-impact turns.
4. Build toward the next safe damage window.
5. Reset the attempt when a key early sequence goes badly rather than dragging out a low-probability run.

## Team construction

For each boss element, maintain three possible concepts when the roster allows:

- **Reliable team** — highest clear consistency
- **Fast team** — more aggressive, shorter fight
- **Ceiling team** — strongest theoretical clear but more dependent on timing or RNG

The reliable team should be the default recommendation for progression.

## Equipment

Recent notable equipment acquisitions tracked in the progression include:

- Karen Hair
- Wizard Hat
- Mermaid Scarf
- Felt Fedora
- Golden Halo

Temperance is also part of the current collection being considered in team planning.

Equipment should be assigned around the role the pet actually performs. A universally strong item can still be the wrong choice if it weakens the pet's specific boss job.

## Stage-band planning

At every new major boss checkpoint:

1. Identify the boss element and mechanic.
2. Build the safest team from the current roster.
3. Identify the one pet investment most likely to improve the next several bosses.
4. Only then spend resources on narrower counters.
5. Record the successful team so the answer is reusable later.

## Hard-mode training

Hard-mode training bosses should be treated as their own optimization problem. A pet that is merely acceptable in normal stage progression may become a priority if it solves an important training matchup.
